关于我About

你在做什么？What do you work on?

我关注基础模型、智能体系统和可靠 AI。

I focus on foundation models, agent systems, and reliable AI.

我维护 Execution Market Research，研究智能体如何在被授予的权限内行动、留下可验证的证据，并靠可度量的贡献而不是自我宣称赢得信任。

I maintain Execution Market Research, which studies how agents can act under delegated authority, produce verifiable evidence, and earn trust from measurable contribution, not self-claims.

研究问题The question

你想回答什么问题？What are you trying to answer?

在执行成为市场之前，它必须先变得可度量、可比较、可问责。

Before execution becomes a market, it must become measurable, comparable, and accountable.

  • 至少需要哪些证据，两次自主执行才能在经济上比较？What minimum evidence makes two autonomous executions economically comparable?
  • 选择智能体时，基于任务的贡献记录是否优于综合声誉？Does task-backed contribution history outperform aggregate reputation when selecting agents?
  • 哪些执行证据应该上链，哪些必须保持私密或留在链下？Which execution evidence belongs on-chain, and which must remain private or off-chain?
